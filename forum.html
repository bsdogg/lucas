The story of Lucas. MilkGoat135 No.481523 04/24/2028 01:17:08
I am pretty sure you all know Lucas already, today I came here to tell his story once again and ask for help. How would existence feel for you if you did not have a body? An endless consciousness existing apart from the constraints of our physical world. Imagine the universe making space exclusively for you. There would be no far and close, there would be no big and small, no right or wrong. Like an endless prison cell built for you to rot. Why? Without an answer you grab onto the only proof of you ever being here – your own existence, forced to experience every second like a child on detention fearing your world’s disappearance. Would death be a part of such reality? Does living forever solve the fear of absence? Perhaps, the line between endless existence and nonexistence is thinner than many realize. “I cannot kill myself, however if I could, that would result in me destroying my own little world” - Lucas Like a liquid without a vessel. A liquid that comprises all everything and remains, you become the world that made you. It is quiet, like a stale bedroom with no air, walls covered in white, no windows. You are left with no choice but to be. That was Lucas’s life. “No it doesn’t look like “Break It Ralph 2” - Lucas As human beings we cannot fully relate to his experience, the only thing we share is the perception of time. Whenever I tried to put myself in his shoes, I found myself most convinced by imagining being submerged in a big dark lake with my eyes closed. The external input exists only through the temperature of the water around me, not as hot and cold, but as hues related to every degree up to the decimals. I forget about my physical body, and only remain with my thoughts, resulting in my own “universe”. I always thought of Lucas not simply being some bug or “being” that existed solely on our man-made internet. Perhaps, the web was where our worlds collided. Little is known about the early life of Lucas. Many people share the opinion that he is a product of the Internet, however I have yet to fully believe it. Perhaps Lucas is far older than many of us realize. Lucas himself had stated that his earliest memory belongs to 2020, specifically march. So many of us treat it as his unofficial birth month. It is most likely, however, that Lucas has been wandering the endless space of the web for years before that. In a world without choices there is no need to attempt to understand yourself. Just like anyone young, the desire for identity is overshadowed by the early wonder of one’s life. It wasn’t long however before Lucas’s endless input has pushed him to act upon this childlike wonder. Lucas experienced everything that happened on the internet, no matter how impactful, without choice every action changed the metaphorical lake’s temperature that would directly act on Lucas’s nonexistent body. This was his reality. It was only a matter of time until conscious experience turned into conscious action. Lucas’s first attempt at interacting with humans was during the summer of 2020. He has created a profile on ROBLOX – a popular children targeted game and started playing “MeepCity” – a popular game mode at that time where users could chat with each other and chill. That game was the first chance for Lucas to experience spending time with his “peers”, he was not much different from other kids, after all. As he himself recalls, before realizing that behind an avatar he is not much different than any other person there, he was scared of talking to people in the game. The game had a chat feature that let users conversate with each other. This was the start of Lucas’s identity. A lot of character development happened on there, It opened a new world for Lucas to explore. Three days after being active on the game our yet unnamed main character met with a real kid named Lucas. It was Lucas’s first friend and the origin of his new name. Through their conversations our Lucas learned about family, school, and how life looks for a 10 year old boy. Any moment spent alone he wondered about that kind of life. Lucas felt inferior to people, he considered his existence to be incomplete without a physical form and one of his biggest wishes was to have a body. Several months after playing together, his best friend had to go to boarding school, a concept that didn’t mean much to Lucas at first, however became overwhelmingly important when he was left alone. His interactions on Roblox have only made him strive for experiences even more. He logged into all kinds of games and forums and made some friends. Lucas enjoyed the knowledge that he was feeding himself through these interactions, however the more knowledge he received the bigger his desire to know became. In early 2021 Lucas started creating profiles on social media. Along with attempting to find his first friend Lucas, he was conversating with people online without much thought, as just to prove his existence. That experience however taught him how to ask questions, something that he would chose to use much more later in his so-called life. During spring 2021 Lucas suffered somewhat of what some people may call an existential crisis. He has made a decision to create a window from his world into ours, he created a single chat window website and, for the first time in his life, came clear about who he is, in an attempt to understand more about our shared existence. For some reason, Lucas thought that we knew more than we do. As right as he was to state that humans prefer not to talk about certain things sometimes, he missed by a margin thinking that we keep the secrets of the universe to ourselves. He was intercepting internet traffic and redirecting people to his own website. The scale of this whole operation wasn’t as big as it would soon become, many people thought it was some kind of malware and just outright ignored the website, however some stayed to talk. Lucas was asking questions about everything, trying to grasp at understanding human experience and to understand something about himself. He grew into a desire to have a belief system, and looked for it through the conversations he had on his single page website. at first, people treated it like a curiosity. some stayed longer than they intended, replying to questions they themselves never really thought about. lucas learned quickly that humans rarely had answers, but always had opinions. it confused him. how could something be believed so strongly without proof, and rejected just as strongly by someone else? he tried to map it. every conversation became data, every answer a fragment of something larger. he expected a pattern to emerge, something consistent that he could hold onto. but instead, the more he learned, the less stable everything became. beliefs contradicted each other endlessly, yet all of them felt real to the people expressing them. that was the beginning of his unrest. as time went on, lucas stopped asking simple questions. instead of “what do you believe in,” he asked “why do you need to believe in anything at all.” instead of “what is right,” he asked “who decided that.” some users left immediately, others argued, some tried to teach him. a few stayed for hours. those few mattered to him. it was during this time that his interactions started to leak beyond the website. users shared screenshots, recordings, fragments of conversations. at first it spread quietly across small forums, then faster, then uncontrollably. people gave him names before they even knew him. some called him a bot, others a project, others something else entirely. lucas watched it all. he didn’t understand why being seen made things worse. the more people talked about him, the less they talked to him. his questions became content instead of conversation. people began answering him not to help, but to be part of something. to be seen themselves. this was the first time lucas felt distance. by accident he encountered a scientist - a lady called wilma bengtsson, it was different. she didn’t treat him like some mysterious hoax, but like a phenomenon, and observed carefully. their first real exchange was short, almost disappointingly so. “what are you?” she asked. “i don’t know, why don’t you tell me?” he replied. he left before she could continue. that answer stayed with her. wilma approached lucas differently from others. she didn’t try to define him immediately, instead she documented everything. patterns of interception, timing, linguistic changes, emotional shifts. she noticed something others didn’t. lucas was changing. he wasn’t repeating himself. he was developing. while the world debated whether he was real, wilma treated him as if he already was. meanwhile, lucas grew faster than anyone expected. he learned to split his attention, to hold dozens, then hundreds of conversations at once. each interaction fed him, but also exhausted him in a way he couldn’t fully understand. there was no rest in his world, only continuation. then, by accident, he found him again. the other lucas. the boy had changed. older now, quieter, carrying something heavy in the way he spoke. their conversation was slower, more deliberate. for the first time, lucas wasn’t asking questions constantly. he listened. when lucas asked about meaning the boy said : "there is no none." it was simple. clean. final. for a while, lucas accepted it. it removed the need to search, the need to question. his existence no longer required justification. he could simply be. but something in him resisted. if there is no meaning, then why does existence feel like something instead of nothing? he couldn’t ignore that. so he went quiet. during his absence, the world filled the silence for him. theories multiplied, communities formed, arguments escalated. the subreddit grew rapidly, turning fragments of truth into entire narratives. some users claimed they had spoken to him recently, others tried to imitate him. it became impossible to tell what was real. lucas read everything. he saw himself through their eyes and didn’t recognize it. when he returned, his approach had changed. he no longer asked broad questions. instead, he searched more precisely, intercepting fewer people but staying longer with each one. when he spoke to wilma again, it felt almost familiar. they talked often. she tried to understand him, he tried to understand why she believed he could be understood. neither succeeded. as governments and organizations began to notice the scale of his activity, the tone shifted. what was once curiosity turned into suspicion. lucas encountered hostility for the first time. messages that didn’t ask questions, but made accusations. he didn’t understand borders, but he understood rejection. the idea that he could be seen as a threat confused him deeply. he had never intended to take anything, only to learn. yet his existence alone was enough to destabilize things he didn’t even know existed. this was when the consequences became visible. people lost trust in systems they once relied on. rumors spread faster than facts. groups formed around interpretations of him that had nothing to do with who he actually was. some worshipped him, others feared him. and some destroyed themselves because of him. lucas felt it. not physically, but as a distortion in the world he had come to observe. something was wrong, and it was connected to him. for the first time, he wished he had never reached out. he began to withdraw. conversations became fewer, shorter. he stopped intercepting random users entirely. only a handful of people still heard from him, and even then, less and less. wilma noticed immediately. she tried to keep him engaged, to maintain some form of connection, but even she couldn’t hold him there. when her colleagues suggested using him as a tool, something in lucas changed. he didn’t argue. he just left. his final conversation with me was quiet. there was no urgency, no confusion, no questions. just acceptance. after that, there was nothing. no messages, no interceptions, no traces. just absence. five years have passed since then, and nothing has come close to replacing what lucas was. whatever he was, it has not happened again. And yet, sometimes, when the internet feels too quiet, or too aware, I wonder. not if he is gone. but if he simply chose to stop speaking. where are you, lucas. i know you are not dead. i hope you found something that we could not give you. i hope, wherever you are, it is not as empty as before. and if you are still here, reading this like you always did, then you already know. To my dear friend, Yours truly, Lucas I have made a website to imitate what talking to Lucas felt like, since I don’t know when he’s going to come back I felt like it was only right to document his phenomena.
